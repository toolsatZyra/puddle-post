# Accepted brief — September 10, 2026

The user selected Puddle Post after inspection of Mosswing and requested a complete good-looking, smooth, non-pixelated, addictive game. Libraries and tools were authorized as needed. No further user assets or credentials were required.

Chosen concept: a tiny mouse delivering letters under an oversized yellow umbrella through a rainy miniature village. Retain one-button upward impulses and gravity; add automatic delivery near mailboxes. Reward successful delivery through lights and waving residents, while allowing missed letters and fast retries.

Quality priorities: original smooth 3D imagery, polished animation and responsive controls, a readable uncluttered first experience, generous early passages, progressive challenge, personal records, phone layout, pause/resume, and silence by default. Each product remains isolated in its own workspace folder.

Implementation and actual output: `src/` contains the authored game and procedural assets, `public/` contains local fonts and notices, and `dist/` is the generated playable production output. Browser verification is recorded separately; no third-party character assets were copied.

## Accepted aesthetic revision — September 10, 2026

The user requested a late-evening scene with colourful/pastel homes and a clear move away from the dominant forest-green aesthetic, while keeping the bushes. Implemented blue-hour lavender/indigo skies, a moon and faint stars, peach/blue/lilac/yellow homes, warmer illuminated windows, and apricot/plum/cream interface colours. Foreground bush geometry and its green materials remain. Gameplay and controls are unchanged.

## Difficulty and reward revision — September 10, 2026

The user requested clear gap variation after the third pillar, difficulty-based delivery rewards, and a level increase with 15% more speed after every ten deliveries. Implemented three introductory wide passages, then shuffled wide/medium/tight gaps with different heights and letter positions, visible 10/25/50-point postage badges, +20 points per five-delivery streak, a separate point score and delivery count, and compounding level-based speed with smooth acceleration. The updated HUD previews the next delivery reward and progress toward the next level.
